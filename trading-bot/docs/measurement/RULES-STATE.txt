  ENTRY LOCATION — proximal edge of the BODY band, WITH ONE KNOWN EXCEPTION
    The edge price reaches first. Long into demand from above -> TOP edge.
    Short into supply from below -> BOTTOM edge. Where a zone is drawn as two
    bands (darker body band, pale wick band), use the BODY band's edge; the
    wick band is not part of the entry geometry.
    Four confirmations:
        2023-10-24 BNB 1D    SHORT   entry on body-band bottom edge, 0.5 px
        2024-06-09 BTC 1D    LONG    entry on box top edge,          0.5 px
        S8 1:02:28 DOGE 2h   SHORT   entry at fraction 1.00
        S8 1:03:38 DOGE 2h   SHORT   entry at fraction 1.00
        S8 1:28:33 SOL 4h    LONG    entry at fraction 0.01 from top
    ONE MEASURED EXCEPTION, and it is real, not an artefact:
        TBOT1 1:09:19 OM 1h  LONG    entry at fraction 0.173 from the zone top
    Re-measured with getImageData, R:R gate passed (2.971 vs printed 2.97), and

    the region (rows 544 and 608) with nothing at the entry row 555 — so there
    is no narrower nested body band that would put this entry on an edge. The

    DO NOT code the edge rule as unconditional until this is explained.

  POSITION TOOL DECODE — for reading any future frame
    Amount = account balance on a $1,000 nominal base ($1,000 derived, not
    assumed). risk = 1000 - Amount(stop leg). reward = Amount(target) - 1000.
    Printed % is entry-relative. Printed R:R = target distance / stop distance.
    Risk is $250 on every frame checked, 2022-11 to 2025-06, eight trades.

  ZONE EDGES — bodies, not wicks. Unchanged across all passes.

  MEASUREMENT GATE — use this on every future frame
    Accept a frame's geometry only if
        (entry_row - target_row) / (stop_row - entry_row)
    reproduces the printed R:R. It caught a genuine error mid-pass (a stop
    label box's bottom being read as the stop line) and it correctly refused
    two frames whose semi-transparent fills and label halos could not be
    resolved closer than ~10 px. Frames that fail the gate are unreadable,
    not estimates.

BLOCKED — ONE DEPENDENCY, THREE QUESTIONS
  Browser caption harvesting is now definitively dead, not merely flaky:
      adjacent cues differ ......... FAIL (21 captures, 1 identical string)
      fresh-reload reproducibility . FAIL (same timestamp, different text
                                     on a different page load)
      capture rate ................. 1 distinct cue in ~20 expected = 5%
  The reproducibility inversion is the dangerous part: the caption element
  latches one REAL cue per page load and binds it to every timestamp queried,
  and which cue it latches varies per run. A harvester would produce genuine
  sentences attached to wrong timestamps — a transcript that reads as clean and
  is confidently wrong.
  Q1 SWING WIDTH, Q2 BREAKOUT % BY TIMEFRAME and Q7 CONFLUENCE COUNT all need
  speech and none of them has moved in five passes. yt-dlp run locally is now
  the only route. Until that happens these three stay open and no amount of
  further frame work will touch them.
