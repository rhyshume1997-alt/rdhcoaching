WHAT THIS IS FOR
Reverse-engineering a chart-marking method into a trading bot rule set. One rule
has four confirmations and one measured exception, and this job tests whether
the exception has a condition behind it or whether the rule is simply wrong.

THE RULE UNDER TEST
Entry sits on the PROXIMAL edge of the zone's body band, meaning the edge price
reaches first. Long into demand from above puts the entry on the top edge, short
into supply from below puts it on the bottom edge.

Confirmations (entry fraction measured from the zone's top edge, so 0.00 is the
top and 1.00 is the bottom):
    2023-10-24 public, BNB 1D    SHORT   1.00, on the bottom edge
    2024-06-09 public, BTC 1D    LONG    0.00, on the top edge
    S8 @ 1:02:28,      DOGE 2h   SHORT   1.00
    S8 @ 1:03:38,      DOGE 2h   SHORT   1.00
    S8 @ 1:28:33,      SOL 4h    LONG    0.01
Exception, re-measured and confirmed real (R:R gate passed, no nested band):
    TBOT1 @ 1:09:19,   OM 1h     LONG    0.173, a sixth of the way inside

THE HYPOTHESIS
He enters at the edge when the zone is VIRGIN, meaning no candle has traded into
it yet, and enters INSIDE the zone when price has already penetrated it. On the
OM chart a capitulation candle appears to have already swept below the zone
before price rallied away. On the SOL and DOGE charts the zones look untouched.
If that distinction holds, the edge rule survives with a condition attached
rather than a hole in it.

THE MEASUREMENT
For each frame below, report:
  1. The zone box's top and bottom, in price, read with getImageData on the raw
     video frame.
  2. Whether ANY candle to the LEFT of the position tool has penetrated the zone.
     Penetrated means a wick or body crossing the proximal edge into the box.
     If yes, give the deepest penetration as a fraction of zone height, and say
     whether it was a wick or a body.
  3. Where the current price sits relative to the zone: above it, below it, or
     inside it.
  4. The entry line's fraction from the zone top, re-measured independently
     rather than taken from the table above.

METHOD NOTES, all of these have cost previous sessions time
  - Seeking while PAUSED often fails to repaint and returns a stale frame from
    the previous seek. Use: seekTo(target - 3), playVideo(), poll currentTime
    until it passes the target, then pauseVideo(). Verify currentTime in output.
  - Playback quality silently collapses to 144p while still reporting "hd720".
    Call setPlaybackQualityRange('hd1080','hd1080') and setPlaybackQuality
    ('hd1080') before and after each seek, and confirm video.videoWidth is 1920
    before trusting any frame.
  - Do NOT read geometry in one magnified view and prices in another and then
    combine them. That has produced an error every single time on this project.
    Read both from the same frame.
  - Calibrate price-per-pixel from the position tool's stop leg, which prints
    its own price span.
  - Validate every frame with the gate
        (entry_row - target_row) / (stop_row - entry_row)
    which must reproduce the printed R:R. It has already caught a stop label
    box's bottom edge being mistaken for the stop line. Frames that fail the
    gate are unreadable, not estimates.
  - Position tool label boxes carry white halos that sit on box edges and cannot
    be resolved closer than about 10 px. Read box edges on columns well clear of
    any label.
  - The tool's "Amount" field is the account balance on a $1,000 nominal base,
    not the risk. risk = 1000 - Amount(stop leg).

Mark anything you cannot establish as unreadable rather than estimating it.
These values go into software and a wrong number is worse than a gap.
