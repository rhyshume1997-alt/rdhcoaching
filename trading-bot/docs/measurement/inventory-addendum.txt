WHAT I GOT WRONG
  1. Two whole classification families. I put the named-lesson Unity streams and
     the standalone "How To..." videos in type B on the reasoning that a title
     naming a concept implies a concept being explained. Both broke under
     sampling. He teaches on live price with real candles and drawn zones,
     several sessions in Replay mode. There is no slide-based content anywhere.
  2. The two sub-60-second clips. I called them D on length alone. Both are
     type A with position tools on screen — they are short because the analysis
     is short, not because they are offcuts.
  3. The overall split. I reported 158 A / 15 B / 7 C / 2 D. The real answer is
     182 A and nothing else. My "87% type A" understated a channel that is
     100% type A.
  The common error: I inferred FORMAT from SUBJECT. A title naming a mechanism
  told me the video would explain the mechanism away from a chart. On this
  channel it never does.
