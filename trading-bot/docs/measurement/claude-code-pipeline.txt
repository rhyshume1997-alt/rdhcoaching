=== TASK 1 — CAPTIONS ===
Browser caption harvesting is dead. It returns real sentences bound to the wrong
timestamps and the binding changes per page load, so it produces a transcript
that reads clean and is confidently wrong. yt-dlp sidesteps it entirely.

  yt-dlp --write-auto-sub --sub-lang en --skip-download --sub-format vtt \
    7HHqibkPmWk TmheqrigTgk 4qruwPWwXmo Qe659NQE_QA sbablVeGaNQ RolQ9uPpse8

Those are six public videos from the channel @arshmeister21. If any fail, tell
me which and why rather than substituting others.

Then search the VTTs and answer three questions. Report exact quotes with
timestamps, and say "not stated" where nothing is found.

  Q1 SWING DEFINITION

     Search: swing high, swing low, pivot, candles either side, higher high,
     lower low, three candles, five candles, confirmed swing

  Q2 MINIMUM BREAKOUT PERCENTAGE BY TIMEFRAME

=== TASK 2 — FRAME MEASUREMENT ===
Pull only the seconds needed, not whole videos:

  yt-dlp --download-sections "*1:09:10-1:09:30" -f "bv*[height<=1080]" \
    --force-keyframes-at-cuts <videoId>

then ffmpeg to extract the exact frame as PNG, then measure with numpy or PIL.
This is strictly better than the browser method: no seek staleness, no silent
quality collapse to 144p, deterministic pixels, and reproducible.

THE QUESTION: entry placement is settled as "the proximal edge of the zone's
body band", meaning the edge price reaches first. Long into demand from above
puts entry on the top edge, short into supply from below puts it on the bottom
edge. Five frames confirm it. One frame breaks it, with the entry 0.173 of the
way inside the zone instead of on its edge.

HYPOTHESIS TO TEST: he enters at the edge when the zone is untouched, and inside
it when price has already traded through the zone.

For each frame report:
  1. Zone box top and bottom in price.
  2. Whether any candle LEFT of the position tool has penetrated the zone. If
     so, how deep as a fraction of zone height, and whether wick or body.
  3. Where current price sits: above, below or inside the zone.
  4. The entry line's fraction from the zone top, measured independently.

VERDICT: does prior penetration predict edge versus inside entry across all six?
If the OM zone turns out untouched, or an edge-entry zone turns out already
penetrated, the hypothesis is dead and the entry rule has a real hole. Say so.

=== MEASUREMENT RULES, LEARNED THE HARD WAY ===
  - Calibrate price per pixel from the position tool's stop leg, which prints
    its own price span. Do not calibrate off axis labels: they get pushed apart
    to avoid overlapping and their positions are not true.
  - Validate every frame with
        (entry_row - target_row) / (stop_row - entry_row) == printed R:R
    A frame failing that gate is unreadable, not an estimate. This gate already
    caught a stop label box's bottom edge being read as the stop line.
  - Position tool label boxes have white halos sitting on box edges. Read edges
    on columns well clear of any label.
  - The tool's Amount field is account balance on a $1,000 nominal base, not
    risk. risk = 1000 - Amount(stop leg), reward = Amount(target) - 1000.
  - Never combine a measurement from one view with a price from another. That
    single mistake produced both wrong answers in this project so far.
