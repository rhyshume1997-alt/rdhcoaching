=== OPERATIONAL NOTES — these cost the last session a lot of time ===
- The channel Videos tab UNDER-RENDERS in the DOM: scrolling loads only ~3 tiles
  and the grid never grows. Do not scrape the DOM. Read window.ytInitialData
  instead, then follow continuations by POSTing to /youtubei/v1/browse with
  ytcfg INNERTUBE_API_KEY + INNERTUBE_CONTEXT and the continuation token. Six
  continuation pages returns all 181.
- Video tiles are lockupViewModel, NOT videoRenderer. Title is at
  metadata.lockupMetadataViewModel.title.content; views and age are in
  metadata...contentMetadataViewModel.metadataRows; duration is only inside
  contentImage overlays — easiest to regex the stringified contentImage for
  "MM:SS" or "H:MM:SS".
- JavaScript tool results are capped around 1000 characters. Chunk any long
  output into slices of ~20 rows, or you will silently lose the tail.
- Do NOT return continuation tokens or hrefs containing query strings from the JS
  tool — the result gets blocked. Stash them on window and return only a boolean.
- Seeking with the player PAUSED often does not repaint; you get a stale frame
  from the previous seek and will happily measure the wrong video. Reliable
  method: seekTo(target - 3), playVideo(), poll currentTime until it passes the
  target, then pauseVideo(). Always verify currentTime in the result.
- Playback quality silently collapses to 144p (256x144) and reports "hd720" while
  doing it. Charts are unreadable there. Call
  setPlaybackQualityRange('hd1080','hd1080') AND setPlaybackQuality('hd1080')
  before and after each seek, and check video.videoWidth is 1920 before trusting
  any frame.
- To read a chart frame clearly, hide the YouTube chrome and blow the player up
  to fill the viewport, then magnify with a CSS transform on the <video> element
  (transformOrigin '0 0' plus translate+scale) rather than the screenshot zoom
  action, which does not magnify past native resolution.
- "Show transcript" is NOT in the "..." menu under a video — that menu only has
  Download and Report. It lives in the expanded description: click "...more",
  then "Show transcript" at the bottom. Captions on this channel are English
  auto-generated (ASR), confirmed on the newest video.
