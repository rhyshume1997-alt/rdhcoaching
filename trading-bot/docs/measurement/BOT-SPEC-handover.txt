TRADING BOT RULE SET — HANDOVER SPECIFICATION
Output of five measurement passes against video frames of a crypto
TA educator's TradingView charts. Everything below is measured from
chart pixels and printed tool labels, not from anything he said.
Read the whole document before writing code. Several plausible
looking rules in here are marked WITHDRAWN and must not be revived.
================================================================

BEFORE YOU WRITE ANY CODE, ASK ME
  1. Language and stack.
  2. Data source: exchange API, CSV history, or something else.
  3. Backtest only, paper, or live.
  4. Which instruments and timeframes to target.
  5. Whether zones are to be detected programmatically or supplied by hand.
     This matters more than it sounds. Every rule below assumes a zone already
     exists. Zone DETECTION is not specified by this document.

--- SETTLED RULES ---

1. ZONE CONSTRUCTION
   A zone box spans candle BODIES, not wicks. Confirmed across every frame in
   all five passes.
   Some zones are drawn as two stacked bands: an inner darker BODY band and an
   outer paler WICK band extending to the wick extremes. Where both exist:
       - entry geometry uses the BODY band only
       - the WICK band is used only when locating the stop
   Measured example (BNB 1D):
       wick band   242.20 to 248.56   (pale)     [C5: read 249.25 corrected]
       body band   237.79 to 242.20   (darker, bordered)  [C5: 237.80 to 242.49]

2. ENTRY PLACEMENT                                    confidence: high
   Entry sits on the PROXIMAL edge of the body band, meaning the edge price
   reaches first.
       long into demand from above   -> TOP edge of the body band
       short into supply from below  -> BOTTOM edge of the body band
   Five confirmations across two directions, four instruments, 2023 to 2025,
   two independent measurement methods. Two of them land within 0.5 pixels of
   the edge.
   ONE KNOWN EXCEPTION, unexplained at time of writing: a long with the entry
   0.173 of the way into the zone rather than on its edge. Re-measured and
   confirmed real. A test is pending on whether prior penetration of the zone
   explains it (edge entry on a virgin zone, inside entry on one price has
   already swept). Until that resolves, treat the edge rule as correct but
   allow an offset parameter defaulting to 0.

3. STOP PLACEMENT                                     confidence: medium
   The stop is placed AT a structural level, not at a computed distance from
   the entry. The level is whichever of these is nearest beyond the zone:
       - the far edge of the zone's wick band
       - the far edge of a separate deeper zone
       - a plain drawn horizontal level
   Implement as: snap the stop to the nearest such level.
   His own placement carries slop of up to about 0.8% of price, on EITHER side
   of the level, and in one case the stop sat INSIDE the band it was nearest
   to. That looks like hand placement, not a rule. Do not model an offset.
   Qualitative constraint that held on every frame measured: the stop is below
   the candle bodies and above the deepest capitulation wick. It does not clear
   the capitulation wick, which is deliberate and repeated.

4. TARGETS                                            confidence: low
   No fixed rule found. Observed reward-to-risk across eight trades:
       1.60, 2.06, 2.13, 2.27, 2.61, 2.97, 3.17, 5.00
       median 2.44, mean 2.73, six of eight fall between 2.0 and 3.2
   Treat target selection as unspecified. If a default is needed, R:R 2.5 sits
   at the centre of what he actually does, but this is a description of his
   behaviour, not a rule he follows.

5. POSITION SIZING                                    NOT DERIVABLE
   His charts show $250 risk on a $1,000 nominal account, unchanged across
   eight trades from 2022-11 to 2025-06. That is 25% per trade and it is a
   teaching template, not a live sizing rule. No frame anywhere shows a real
   account size or a real percentage.
   Derive position size from the operator's own risk budget and the stop
   distance. Do not import 250, do not import 25%.

--- WITHDRAWN. DO NOT REVIVE THESE ---

  "Stop sits roughly 0.5x the zone height below the zone bottom."
      Came from three samples reading 0.479, 0.487, 0.582. It is an artefact of
      which band was nominated as "the zone". The same frames yield anywhere
      from 0.042 to 4.145 depending on that choice, a factor of 99.

  "Risk per trade is a fixed $750."
      Misinterpretation of the position tool's Amount field. It is $250, and it
      is a template regardless.

  "The stop clears the structure by a fixed gap."
      Tested as pixels, as percent of price, as percent of stop distance and as
      a fraction of zone height. Inconsistent on all four, and the sign is not
      even stable. Dead.

  "Entry is at the 50% line of the zone."
      A caption cue suggested this. Measurement shows the 50% line is neither
      the entry nor the DCA level. It sits near the body band's midpoint and
      has no established role.

--- OPEN, HANDLE AS PARAMETERS ---
  These three could not be recovered from chart pixels because he states them
  aloud rather than drawing them. Do not hard code a guess. Make each a config
  value and sweep it in backtest. The operator's own data is a better authority
  here than the source material.
      SWING_WIDTH            candles either side of a pivot that must not
                             exceed it. Weak single-frame evidence for 4.
                             Sweep 2 to 5.
      MIN_BREAKOUT_PCT       by timeframe. He specifies 15m, 30m, 1H and 1D
                             somewhere in his material but nothing was found
                             for 2H, 4H, 8H or 12H across five passes. It is
                             possible he never specifies them. Sweep.
      CONFLUENCE_THRESHOLD   count of overlapping marked objects required
                             before taking a setup. One clean setup showed 5
                             objects converging. His own threshold is unknown.
                             Sweep 2 to 5.

--- READING FUTURE CHARTS ---
  If more frames get measured later, the TradingView position tool decodes as:
      Amount on the stop leg    = account balance after the loss, on a $1,000
                                  nominal base. risk   = 1000 - Amount
      Amount on the target leg  = balance after the win.  reward = Amount - 1000
      printed %                 = entry-relative
      printed R:R               = target distance / stop distance
  The $1,000 base is derived, not assumed: solving for it from three frames in
  three different years returns exactly 1000.00 each time.
  Validate any frame reading with
      (entry_row - target_row) / (stop_row - entry_row)  ==  printed R:R
  A frame that fails that gate is unreadable, not an estimate.

--- PROVENANCE ---
  Five passes. Sources: the educator's public YouTube channel (@arshmeister21,
  182 videos, all live chart analysis) and the operator's own private course
  recordings. Measurements are getImageData pixel reads of raw video frames
  with price-per-pixel calibrated from the position tool's printed stop leg.
  Two figures in earlier drafts were wrong and were corrected: the risk amount
  and the stop fraction. Both errors came from the same cause, which was
  combining a measurement taken in one magnified view with a price read in
  another. Anything measured that way in future should be treated as suspect.
