## 1. Scope
Final session of the course, taught live into a bearish, news-driven tape (FOMC + Israel/Iran headlines). It covers how to trade 4H-and-lower scalps: how he identifies SFPs, trend-line breakdowns, order blocks and supply/demand on low timeframes, where entry/DCA/stop/TP1-3 go, and how to pick which coins are even scalpable (volatility, volume, beta coins). It also delivers a running commentary on five live scalp charts (AR, DOGE, AIOZ, PENDLE, SOL) plus one spot swing idea (CRV), and repeatedly frames low-timeframe trading as the weakest part of his own edge.

## 2. Definitions
- **Swing failure / SFP** — price wicks beyond a prior swing high (or low) and the candle CLOSES back below (or above) it; if it closes beyond the level, it is not an SFP. `[00:22:32]` `[00:24:46]`
- **Weak SFP** — an SFP where the two highs/candles are "way too close to each other"; he will not trade it. `[00:16:46]` `[00:23:38]`
- **Equal highs / equal lows** — equal highs are bearish (rejection from the prior high), equal lows are bullish. Note: he treats an equal high as tradable rejection even without a clean sweep, which sits awkwardly with his own SFP definition. `[00:16:11]`
- **Reclaim** — price simply closes back above a lost level. Nothing more. `[00:30:37]` `[00:38:14]`
- **Flip** — break of a level, then retest, then hold, with multiple bounces confirming it as support/resistance. A flip is strictly stronger than a reclaim; the two are "two different things". `[00:36:33]` `[00:37:07]`
- **Deviation** — price reclaims a level but never flips it, so the move below/above was just a wick outside the range. `[01:05:19]` `[01:18:47]`
- **Market structure break** — structure breaks when a previous lower high is taken out (bull case) or previous lows are taken out (bear case); after a lower high forms, theory says you go make a lower low. `[00:19:09]` `[01:17:19]`
- **Last higher low (his version)** — he distinguishes the "last technical higher low" from "my higher low", which is the deeper one carrying demand-zone/major-support confluence. Deliberately non-textbook: he stays bullish until HIS level breaks, not the technical one. `[00:04:35]` `[00:07:28]` `[00:08:04]`
- **Bearish engulfing candle** — side-by-side candles where the red candle's body engulfs the previous green candle's body; expects continuation down. `[00:47:58]`
- **Order block (his usage)** — the candle/zone from which a move originated, drawn wick-to-highs; once "completely filled" it is dead. `[00:34:48]` `[01:14:50]`
- **Supply zone (valid)** — must be concise; a zone that is "way too wide and not concise" does not qualify. `[01:18:08]`
- **Quasimodo** — higher high, higher low, higher high, lower low, double lower lows back into the rejection area, with a neckline. `[00:10:53]` `[00:11:31]`
- **Flag** — the consolidation must be flat or sloping against the impulse; if it slopes up it is a rising wedge or a rising parallel channel instead. `[01:10:30]` `[01:11:10]`
- **Rising parallel channel** — neutral, neither bullish nor bearish. `[01:11:44]`
- **Capitulation** — one giant wick down, NOT a slow grind down. `[00:14:23]`
- **Doomsday limits** — pre-placed limit bids at high-timeframe support levels, set in advance for capitulation wicks. `[00:14:23]`
- **Beta coins** — ecosystem coins that track a major but move further (SOL: POPCAT, WIF, MYRO; ETH: LDO, OP); higher returns, lower liquidity. `[01:42:20]` `[01:43:29]`
- **Barcoding** — chart that looks like a supermarket barcode; a coin to stay away from. `[01:04:42]`
- **Bullish divergence** — price makes lower lows while RSI makes higher lows. He explicitly says he does not teach or use it, then uses it twice. `[01:25:35]` `[01:50:29]`
- **RSI zones (his version, non-textbook)** — below 50 = oversold; above 80 = highly overbought; 50 = neutral / "reset". Textbook is 30/70. `[01:25:35]` `[01:26:09]`
- **Price discovery** — above all-time highs; only then does he use extension fibs. `[01:30:18]`
- **Golden pocket** — used interchangeably with the 0.786 fib in this session. `[01:07:57]` `[01:14:50]`
- **Crypto rotation cycle** — BTC leads the rally, then ETH and large caps, then midcaps, then small caps; at all-time-high breaks alts do nothing for a few weeks up to about a month. `[00:12:07]` `[00:12:40]`

## 5. Entry model

**Setup identification (scalp, 4H and below):**
1. Establish higher-timeframe structure first on daily/4H: count highs and lows, switch to a line chart when the count is unclear `[00:18:34]` `[00:33:06]`. Bearish structure = lower highs + lower lows `[00:18:34]`.
2. Plot support/resistance, supply/demand zones, order blocks and any valid (3-touch) trend line on the higher timeframe, then drop to 15m/1H to place orders `[00:51:47]` `[00:52:21]`.
3. Require stacked confluence at the level: SR point + supply/demand zone + order block + trend line + fib (0.786/golden pocket) + CME gap where available `[00:40:06]` `[00:43:00]` `[00:53:41]` `[01:14:50]` `[01:21:13]`.
4. Filter the coin: volatile, high-cap, high-volume; no barcoding charts, no unknown low-cap gainers `[00:57:14]` `[00:58:58]` `[01:04:42]`.

**Order placement (limit ladder, shorts):** first entry is the LIGHT one at the nearer level (the SR point / order block), and the single DCA is the HEAVIER one at the outer level (top of resistance / top of the supply zone), so the blended average lands between them `[00:21:24]` `[00:48:34]` `[01:02:28]` `[01:19:57]`. Longs mirror this: light entry at the order block, heavier DCA at the support/demand line below `[00:55:33]` `[01:28:33]`. Maximum one DCA on a scalp, zero is acceptable `[01:02:28]`.

**Alternative entries:**
- SFP entry on the close of the sweeping candle; if missed, limit at the previous swing low/high `[00:16:46]` `[00:23:38]` `[01:26:45]`.
- Post-structure-break entry at the NEXT lower high, not on the breaking candle `[00:25:58]` `[01:17:19]`.
- Trend-line breakdown: enter on break, or safer on the retest of the broken line — single entry only, no DCA `[00:28:18]` `[00:31:44]`.
- Breakout-retest-hold (flip) long at the retested level; he prefers flips to reclaims `[00:37:41]` `[01:16:04]`.
- Deviation short: wait to see whether price reclaims a lost support; if it is only a deviation, short from the higher level with the DCA at the top of resistance `[01:18:47]` `[01:19:23]`.

## 6. Stops
- Default placement is beyond the wick of the zone being traded: above the wick for shorts, below the wick for longs `[00:48:34]` `[00:49:09]` `[01:19:57]`.
- For a long taken off an SFP, the stop goes below whatever wick the SFP created `[00:49:45]` `[01:12:56]`.
- To tighten a stop, drop to a lower timeframe and use the nearest wick there instead of the higher-timeframe one `[01:21:46]`.
- Trend-line breakdown trades have effectively no hard stop; the exit is a candle CLOSE back above the line and the trade must be watched manually `[00:28:52]` `[00:30:04]` `[00:32:20]`.
- Leverage always needs a hard invalidation because of liquidation risk; spot uses a price alert plus a close-below-and-flip check instead `[01:35:25]` `[01:36:00]`.
- A stop is "too wide" implicitly when the zone is too wide: he rejects supply zones that are "way too wide and not concise" `[01:18:08]`, and rejects an order-block long where the zone forces a poor stop `[00:47:20]`. On AIOZ/PENDLE he only takes the play with a "very very tight stop" `[01:06:41]` `[01:14:14]` `[01:21:46]`. No numeric max stop distance is ever given.

## 7. Take profit and trade management
- Three TPs on scalps, placed at successive structural levels of support (shorts) or resistance (longs); not at fixed R multiples `[00:49:09]` `[00:49:45]` `[00:31:44]` `[01:19:57]`.
- If the DCA fills, the original entry becomes an SR point: TP1 is re-mapped to that level and the remaining TPs are pushed out based on price action `[01:03:03]`.
- On a double top after a large run: take partial profit and move stop to break-even / risk-free `[00:11:31]` `[00:12:07]`.

## 8. Invalidation and re-entry
- **Order block:** dead once completely filled; do not replay it — find the newer order block `[00:34:48]`.
